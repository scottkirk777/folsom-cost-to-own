Folsom Mello-Roos, district by district

All of Folsom Ranch is in CFD 18, and the Folsom Ranch sample bills we checked showed $1,565 to $4,344 a year in Mello-Roos and districts. North of Highway 50, Empire Ranch and Lakeview Oaks (CFD 10) and The Parkway Phase II (CFD 14) pay a facilities Mello-Roos. Hillcrest pays a maintenance district, CFD 12, and some older areas pay smaller maintenance charges.

  • Highest on our sample bills: White Rock Springs Ranch, $4,344 a year.
  • When the bonds end: 2032 to 2059 for districts with published dates. Services and maintenance taxes can keep going after that.
  • Before you buy: the seller must give you a Notice of Special Tax for each district.

How to read the Mello-Roos on a tax bill

  1. Get the parcel number. It's on the listing, the seller's disclosures, or the county assessor's parcel viewer.
  2. Open the county tax bill. Search the parcel on the Sacramento County property tax site.
  3. Find the direct levies. Each district is its own line, with a name like "Folsom CFD 18" and a dollar amount.
  4. Add it to the base tax. That sum, plus any HOA dues, is your yearly tax and HOA cost. Your mortgage and insurance come on top.

Common questions

What is Mello-Roos in Folsom?
A special tax from a Community Facilities District (CFD). It pays for roads, parks, pipelines and other public facilities in a newer area. It shows as its own line in the direct levies section of the Sacramento County tax bill.
Which Folsom neighborhoods have Mello-Roos?
All of Folsom Ranch, south of Highway 50, is in CFD 18. Russell Ranch, Mangini Ranch, White Rock Springs Ranch and the Toll Brothers areas also have their own districts. North of Highway 50, Empire Ranch and Lakeview Oaks are in CFD 10, The Parkway Phase II is in CFD 14 and Hillcrest (Broadstone Unit 3) is in CFD 12. Many other north-side neighborhoods pay only a landscape and lighting charge. The tables list them all.
Does Mello-Roos go away?
The facilities tax ends when the bonds are paid. Published final bond dates run from Sep 1, 2032 (CFD 14, The Parkway Phase II) to Sep 1, 2059 (CFD 18). Newer districts can run longer: CFD 20-1 through 2064-65 and proposed CFD 24 through 2085-86. Services taxes, like the CFD 23 services tax, can continue after the bonds are paid.
Does the seller have to tell me about Mello-Roos?
Yes. California Civil Code 1102.6b requires the seller to make a good faith effort to get a Notice of Special Tax from each district and give it to you. If you get it after you sign the purchase contract, you can cancel within 3 days of getting it in person, or 5 days after it is mailed.
How do I find the Mello-Roos on a specific Folsom house?
Get the parcel number, look it up on the Sacramento County property tax bill site, and read the direct levies section. Each district is its own line, with a name and a dollar amount.
