What is actual and what is an estimate: the base rate and the district charges are copied from the sample bill. The base tax shown elsewhere on this site is an estimate: the base rate times the neighborhood's median sale price, because a buyer's base tax follows the price they pay. The yearly totals add that estimate, the actual district charges, and the median HOA dues where at least half the sales had an HOA. Each part is rounded to the dollar before it is added. Mello-Roos depends on home size and type, so a different home on the same street can pay a different amount.

Sales and HOA data

MetroList sold single-family homes in ZIP 95630, the 12 months from Nov 1, 2025 to Oct 31, 2026: 762 sales. Neighborhoods come from the MetroList subdivision field. About 6 in 10 sales had no usable name there, so most of those were placed by their assessor map page, matched to labeled sales on the same or a nearby page. Treat neighborhood lines as close, not exact. A neighborhood needs at least 5 sales to show a number.

How the sales were counted

Pulled from MetroList on Nov 4, 2026. Included: sold single-family listings in the City of Folsom, ZIP 95630, that closed from Nov 1, 2025 to Oct 31, 2026. That is 762 MLS numbers, each counted once. Six homes sold twice in the year, and both sales count. Prices are close prices.

Neighborhoods: 287 sales had a subdivision name that matched a neighborhood. 328 had a blank or unmatched name and were placed by their county assessor map page, using the neighborhood most labeled sales on that page, or on the nearest labeled page in the same map book. The other 147 could not be placed with confidence and are grouped as Other Folsom neighborhoods.

Based on information from MetroList Services, Inc. All information is deemed reliable but not guaranteed and should be independently verified.
